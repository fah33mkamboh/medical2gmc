About Medicine Unit-II

Clinical medicine strengthened by academic excellence.

The Department of Medicine Unit-II at Ghulam Muhammad Mahar Medical College, Sukkur is a dedicated academic and clinical department committed to high standards of patient care, postgraduate training, bedside teaching and evidence-based medicine.

The department provides comprehensive evaluation and management of acute and chronic medical illnesses while maintaining a strong emphasis on systematic clinical assessment, diagnostic reasoning, appropriate investigations, treatment and continuity of care.

Alongside its clinical responsibilities, Medicine Unit-II provides an active learning environment for postgraduate trainees and medical students through seminars, case discussions, bedside teaching, journal reviews and structured academic activities.

Our Approach

Medicine Unit-II integrates clinical experience with contemporary medical knowledge. Every patient encounter is also an opportunity for observation, critical thinking, teaching and continued professional development.


Our goal is to develop clinicians who combine strong diagnostic skills, compassionate patient care and sound evidence-based decision-making.

Clinical Services

Comprehensive clinical care under one medical unit.

Medicine Unit-II provides inpatient, outpatient and specialized clinical services with emphasis on careful assessment, appropriate investigation and evidence-based management.

01

Indoor Medical Services

Inpatient evaluation, investigation, monitoring and treatment of patients requiring admission for acute and chronic medical illnesses.

02

Outpatient Department

Clinical consultation, diagnostic assessment, treatment, follow-up and long-term management of medical conditions.

03

High Dependency Unit

Focused observation and closer clinical monitoring for patients requiring a higher level of medical supervision and management.

04

Endoscopy Suite

Dedicated facilities for upper gastrointestinal endoscopic evaluation as part of the department's diagnostic services.

05

Colonoscopy

Lower gastrointestinal endoscopic assessment supporting the diagnosis and evaluation of gastrointestinal disorders.

06

Clinical Teaching

Bedside teaching and case-based learning focused on clinical examination, diagnostic reasoning and practical patient management.

Academic Medicine

Training the next generation of physicians.

Postgraduate education is a central component of Medicine Unit-II. Clinical work is integrated with continuous academic discussion, research awareness and structured teaching.

Postgraduate Seminar Room

A dedicated environment for postgraduate teaching, seminars, presentations, case discussions and academic meetings.

Case Presentations

Structured clinical discussions designed to strengthen history-taking, examination, differential diagnosis and management planning.

Journal & Evidence Review

Review of contemporary medical literature to encourage evidence-based practice and critical appraisal of medical research.

Research & Professional Development

Encouraging research awareness, scientific discussion, postgraduate development and continuous improvement in clinical practice.
