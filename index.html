About Medical Unit-II

Patient care, clinical training and academic medicine.

Medical Unit-II serves as a clinical and teaching unit of GMC Teaching Hospital Sukkur, supporting patient care, postgraduate training, ward-based learning and modern internal medicine practice.

Head of Department

Professor Dr. Saleh Muhammad Channa

Head of Department, Medicine Unit-II · GMC Teaching Hospital Sukkur

Department Focus

Clinical excellence with structured teaching.

The unit is designed around consultant-led patient care, postgraduate education, bedside discussion, OPD services and multidisciplinary clinical learning.
